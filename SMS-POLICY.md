# Bus Ridership demo: SMS policy

Bus Ridership is a personal prototype run by Cole Paris. It sends text alerts
when a test student taps an RFID card on a bus reader. It is not a commercial
service, and no real students are involved: every "student" is a made-up test
user.

## Opt-in

- The only person who receives texts is the account owner, Cole Paris, on his
  own mobile phone.
- He opts in by adding his own number to the prototype's private settings file.
  The prototype has no sign-up form, and no one else's number can be added.
- No phone numbers are bought, imported or collected from anyone else.

## Terms and conditions

- **What you get:** automated alerts saying a test student got on or off a
  bus, and at what time. Example: "Bus Ridership demo: Esme Reyes got on
  School Bus #5 at 7:42 AM. Reply STOP to opt out."
- **How often:** only when a card is tapped, usually a few messages a day.
  Some days have none.
- **Opt out:** reply **STOP** at any time and you will get no more messages.
  Reply **START** to opt back in.
- **Help:** reply **HELP**, or email cole.paris@verkada.com.
- **Cost:** message and data rates may apply.
- **No marketing:** the prototype never sends advertising or promotional
  content.
- Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

## Privacy policy

- **What we store:** the recipient's phone number, in a private settings file
  on the owner's computer. It is never committed to the code repository.
- **What we don't do:** phone numbers and opt-in consent are never sold,
  rented or shared with third parties for marketing.
- **Who sees it:** Twilio, which delivers the texts, receives the phone number
  and message text for that purpose only.
- **Student data:** all student names are made-up test users. No real student
  information is collected, stored or sent.
- **Removal:** reply STOP or email cole.paris@verkada.com, and the number is deleted
  from the settings file.
